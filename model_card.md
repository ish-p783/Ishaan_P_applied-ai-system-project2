## What are the limitations or biases in your system? The limitations are that the chatbot is keyword-sensitive and may struggle not to read certain wording. 
## Could your AI be misused, and how would you prevent that? The AI could be treated as a vet in situations the user tells the AI that their pet is experiencing a medical emergency, in which the guardrails are engaged to prevent the AI to offer advice and help beyond its scope, as a real vet is more suitable to assist with medical emergencies.
## What surprised you while testing your AI's reliability? describe your collaboration with AI during this project. Identify one instance when the AI gave a helpful suggestion and one instance where its suggestion was flawed or incorrect. I was surprised by the chatbot ability to properly engage certain restrictions and using RAG to provide even better responses. The helpful suggestion was when I said Rex was vey hyper lately and the AI suggested a routine that was fit the time constraints while providing high-energy activities. The suggestion I felt wasn't handled well was when the test case said Rex threw up twice and the AI gave advice, but I felt these situations are best to be handled by the vet or a medical professional with animals.
